A bit about me

Journalist by training.
Content strategist by practice.

I work on the structures behind the words: how information is organized, how standards get used, and how teams keep content useful as it grows.

My work has taken me through healthcare and financial services, from McKesson to Charles Schwab. These days, that includes figuring out where AI helps with content work and where it needs a human editor.

I have an MA in Professional Writing from Carnegie Mellon. I also write poetry. There’s room for both.

Core practice
Content strategy, information architecture, UX writing, voice and tone, content governance.
Tools of the trade
Research, content audits, Adobe Analytics, Figma, Contentful, Drupal, and AI-assisted editorial workflows.
