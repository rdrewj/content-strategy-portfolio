A bit about me

Writer by training.
Content strategist by practice.

My work connects individual writing decisions to the systems that keep content useful: information architecture, editorial standards, and review processes.

At McKesson and Charles Schwab, I’ve helped teams turn complex source material into clear guidance. I now apply that experience to AI-assisted editing, with particular attention to evidence and human review.

I have an MA in Professional Writing from Carnegie Mellon.

Core practice
Content strategy, information architecture, UX writing, voice and tone, content governance.
Tools of the trade

Ducks

Harried by crows, children,
Small and large dogs.

At night we huddled close
In a wooden box by the pond—
It was our universe.

And now,
As big as crows,
We fly above it all.
