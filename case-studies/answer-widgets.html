Case Study

Scaling self-service at Schwab: Answer Widgets

I built the content inventory, search-term mapping, and QA process that helped scale an existing search feature from a 50-widget pilot to more than 500 answers.

Organization

Charles Schwab

Scope

Client and prospect search experiences

Timeline

2021 launch; reporting through February 2022

Role

Content strategy — structure, taxonomy, QA

What I owned: Content inventory, FAQ selection, organization by experience, search-term mapping, sprint structure, and content QA. The widget interface was already in place when I joined; my work focused on scaling its content.

The problem

A 500-widget target without the planned automation

When I joined Schwab in spring 2021, Answer Widgets displayed FAQs directly in search results. The pilot had 50 widgets, with a goal of reaching 500 by year’s end through automated FAQ imports.

When that automation did not materialize, I assembled the inventory and prepared the content for manual delivery.

Each answer needed to match the words clients actually searched for.

The strategy

Build the master list. Get the keywords right.

I requested a report of all client and prospect pages from the Drupal team, then filtered for accordion components, the CMS pattern used for FAQs. I reviewed those candidates and selected the answers relevant to search.

I organized the selected FAQs by experience area, including trading, wires, beneficiaries, and taxes. Using Adobe Analytics search data, I mapped the terms clients entered to the corresponding widgets.

The project presentation records the selection and mapping method, but does not preserve individual query-to-widget mappings.

The process

Deliver and check 50 widgets every two weeks

I organized delivery into biweekly batches of 50 widgets, from content preparation through development and QA. At the end of each sprint, I checked keyword mappings, formatting, and grammar in the implemented experience.

Measurement notes and source limits

The presentation's narrative reports 557 widgets, while its chart shows 555 from November through January and 557 in February. A chart note also refers to 550 completed by November. I use “500+” for the launch milestone because the exact November count is inconsistent.

The project summary reports 605K expansions by the end of 2021. The analytics slide uses the label “views” and flags Adobe tracking issues in June and July. These records support reporting engagement, but not a precise causal estimate of the expansion program's impact.

The presentation notes seasonal search spikes in February and March. Reduced support-call volume was an intended benefit; no measured reduction or task-completion result is included.

What came next

Keep the answers current

After launch, the library was audited twice to address issues from a site-wide design-system update. Business stakeholders helped develop new widgets for tax season and product launches, including Thematic Investing. The roadmap included renewed automation, Schwab Assistant integration, and a way for clients to flag content gaps.
